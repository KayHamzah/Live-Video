# Kay short video rules

These apply to every short video made for Kay from this repo.

- **Mandatory end card:** every short ends with a 2.5s black end card: small grey spaced label "TIP DAPUR", a short white line, then bold white text "Follow Kay sekarang untuk lebih banyak tips". `scripts/render2.py` adds it automatically; never remove it, and don't add a separate CTA overlay on top of the video.
- Final length about 30–60s including the end card; Kay is fine with a little over 60s when the content needs it.
- Style: 9:16 1080×1920, black/white text only, Inter Bold for hook and title, Inter Medium for subtitles, hook in the lower cabinet area for the first 3s (never covering the face or product).
- Subtitles are hand-corrected Malay. Write the brand as "Cooven".
